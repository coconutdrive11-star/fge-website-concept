FAQ

Questions? We've got you covered.

Can I invite you to my event?
Yes — we pay you a store fee to set up. Your guests buy from us directly. You pay us nothing.
What brands do you offer for private orders?
Steers, Debonairs Pizza, and Fishaways. You can choose one or mix brands.
Where do you operate for private orders?
Gauteng only — specifically the Benoni and Boksburg area. Contact us to check if we cover your location.
Where do you operate for events?
Gauteng for event sign-ups.
How far in advance must I book a private order?
Minimum 24 hours notice. For large orders (200+ guests), 1–2 days in advance. Payment must be received before we begin preparation.
Is there a minimum number of guests for a private order?
100 guests minimum. Smaller functions are subject to a minimum charge equivalent to 100 guests.
Do we need to provide power?
No. We carry a generator.
What's included in the price?
Staff, gas, generator, setup and breakdown.
